What We Deliver

Three core services across the waste-to-fuel value chain organized simply: project setup, plant deployment, and industrial fuel linkage.

Project Development & Feasibility

Identification of suitable waste processing technologies, plant capacities, pre-feasibility studies, and DPR support for municipal and industrial clients.

Technology Deployment & Plant Operations

Design, installation, commissioning, training, and operational support for complete waste processing plants and RDF infrastructure.

AFX Fuel Supply to Industries

Long-term RDF offtake support for cement, power, and steel industries where alternative fuel demand is strong and scalable.

Integrated Waste-To-Fuel Process

A modern mechanical treatment system designed to minimize manual handling and maximize automated separation efficiency.

01. RECEPTION

Reception & Tipping

Incoming municipal waste intake and inspection compliance screening.

02. SEPARATION

Metal & Material Separation

Extraction of ferrous, non-ferrous metals, plastics, and glass recyclables.

03. SHREDDING

Shredding & Drying

Primary size reduction of material and moisture content reduction.

04. PELLETIZATION

RDF Pelletization

Forming standardized high-grade RDF fuel pellets for thermal industries.
