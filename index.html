Project Aim

To develop an AI-powered industrial waste exchange platform that connects industries generating waste with industries that can use the same waste as raw material[cite: 1]. The project aims to reduce industrial pollution, promote sustainable manufacturing, minimize landfill waste, lower production costs, and support the circular economy[cite: 1].

Key Objectives

  • To reduce industrial waste sent to landfills[cite: 1].
  • To promote recycling and reuse of industrial by-products[cite: 1].
  • To reduce manufacturing costs via alternative raw materials[cite: 1].
  • To support India's Circular Economy Mission[cite: 1].
The Industrial Challenge

Problem Statement

Every year, industries produce millions of tonnes of plastic, metal, textile, glass, food-processing, and chemical waste[cite: 1]. Most of this waste is disposed of through landfilling or incineration, causing environmental pollution and increasing disposal costs[cite: 1].

At the same time, many industries purchase expensive raw materials that could be replaced by recyclable industrial waste[cite: 1]. Due to the absence of a centralized platform, these industries are unable to identify suitable partners for resource exchange[cite: 1].
